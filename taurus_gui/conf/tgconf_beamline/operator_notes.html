Beamline operator panel

One tab per device. Each tab shows the device's State and Status, its values, a trend, and buttons for its commands. The status bar at the bottom right shows every device's State, whichever tab is open: orange for ALARM, red for FAULT or NO CONTACT. The green light next to it is the panel's heartbeat: if it stops blinking, the panel is hung.

Value colors: green is normal, orange is past a warning or alarm level. Hatched rows have no value: the device does not answer. The trend shows this session only; for history, and for anything before the device was last out of contact, use the archive.

Reset magnet (ramp to 0 A) ramps the magnet to 0 A from wherever it is, and makes 0 A its memorized setpoint, so it stays at 0 A after a restart of the device. It asks for confirmation first; Cancel is the default, so pressing Enter does nothing.

Vacuum -- linac/vacuum/gauge1

ItemMeaningLimits
StateON: pressure normal. ALARM: the pressure is past a level in Limits; you can still act. FAULT: no contact with the gauge; Status says why.
Vacuum PressurePressure read from the gauge; the trend is logarithmic.warning above 0.1 mbar; alarm above 0.5 mbar
Pump StatusGreen: pump on. Read only; use the buttons.

Pump ON starts the pump, without confirmation. Pump OFF stops it after confirmation (Cancel is the default): the pressure then stops falling. The device remembers the last command and restores it when the device restarts. After the gauge itself restarts the pump can be off: check Pump Status and press Pump ON.

A device out of contact

While a device does not answer, its rows are hatched, its State in the status bar reads NO CONTACT (or FAULT, with the reason in Status), and its buttons are disabled. Everything returns by itself when the device is back.

If a device does not answer when the panel starts, its tab has a warning sign and names the device and the reason. The panel checks again every 5 s and builds the tab as soon as the device answers; Retry now checks at once. The other tabs work meanwhile.
