Beamline operator panel

Tab 1 shows the quadrupole magnet power supply linac/magnet/q1. Values and states use Taurus's colors: green is normal, yellow a warning, red an alarm, and the trend is gray.

Reset

Ramps the magnet to 0 A from wherever it is, and makes 0 A its memorized setpoint, so it stays at 0 A after a restart of the device. It asks for confirmation first; Cancel is the default, so pressing Enter does nothing.

No contact

If the panel loses contact with the magnet, its values turn hatched with dashes, Status shows None, and the setpoint cannot be written. Everything returns by itself when the device is back. If the device does not answer when the panel starts, the panel names it and offers Retry.

Not on this screen

Ramp rate, polling, archiving and the Init command are expert settings. Use Jive for diagnostics.
